# Quality contract v2

## English source names and display QA

Set and card titles in the delivered result are English, even for a non-English
producer brief. Match the actual object or readable scene hook. The 160 reference
labels are one or two words (3–15 characters); this is an observed guide, not an
original hard character limit. Prefer concise names rather than poetic sentences.
Inspect English labels in the actual report nameplate with the mockup font stated.
No clipping, ellipsis or unreadably small type. Check the five-column final view
and the neutral view. Do not bake names, stars or frames into source art.
Localizations and localization QA are out of scope; source-English fitting is in scope.

## Ideas and planning

Start from an activity, situation or place. Distinguish interpretations by what
the player discovers there, not only by palette or title. Ten cards should supply
different recognisable contributions. Ordinary props are welcome when they help
read the theme; do not demand novelty on every card or invent reference quotas.

Give each card a faithful concept passport: canonical subject, family, role,
visible hook, visible evidence, silhouette and whether it anchors the genre.
Changing the passport cannot make two identical subjects different. Compare
synonyms, functions and silhouettes in the actual scenes. A hook explained only
in a caption is not visible interest. Prefer large physical relationships to
tiny screens, labels or effects. Check world logic, including how an unusual
object works. Give each object its own materials and composition.

Declare recurring identity only where useful. Choose the best full-view source
card explicitly; secondary appearances must wait for that source. Preserve the
same invariants across camera, scale and lighting changes. A background glimpse
must not become the identity master. Existing collection canon stays versioned.

## Idea acceptance

Read the complete 3–5 variants, not only a title list. For each questionable card
name a neighbour, overlap or concrete missing visual cue. Check theme, constraints,
backgrounds, semantic diversity, variant differences, canon, visible interest,
world logic and fidelity of the passport to the art scene. Familiar helmets,
boots and cups are not defects merely because they are familiar. A set of ten
near-identical tools needs revision even when its role labels differ.

## Image acceptance

Inspect the original style references, actual full candidate and 108×120 preview.
Evaluate subject/geometry separately from general beauty: visible part count,
attachment, mechanism, focal silhouette, materials, lighting, background category,
canon and forbidden content. Locate counted parts; do not require hidden far-side
wheels to be separately visible. Exact contents such as three samples are still
requirements. A beautiful four-wheel car cannot pass a six-wheel-rover brief.

An edit must fix the stated defect AND preserve approved geometry. Check for new
attachments or identity drift. Text-only draft workers cannot demonstrate visual
canon preservation. Judge object materials independently: satin paint, rubber,
glass and cloth should not all become glossy plastic. Distinguish original
game-card UI from celestial stars and gold-coloured objects.

Reserve 6–8% of native image width on each side beyond the MAIN contours. Check
the widest lower skirt/hem, elbow, ear and hoof, not only heads. For multi-actor
scenes start with a wider camera so the full main group fits safely. A supporting
handrail intentionally exiting the frame is not a cropped focal silhouette.
On framing feedback zoom out/reduce the WHOLE main group within the same native
canvas; shifting an elbow while the widest skirt still hits the edge is not a
fix. Preserve identities, coherent anatomy and the successful physical action.
Inspect the full native contour and 108x120 preview again after the edit. Never
substitute programmatic padding, export cropping or weaker acceptance gates.
Keep the assigned background category: abstract categories stay abstract; do not
invent a room, floor or horizon to make framing space. Category 1 retains floating
separation from its detached shadow rather than gaining a supporting surface.
Respect configured aspect ratio. If evidence is too small or unavailable, use
uncertain; do not invent full-resolution inspection from a browser thumbnail.

## Findings and retries

Every failed criterion has a blocking finding with exact targets, visible or
textual evidence, and a targeted fix. A pass cannot contain blockers. Minor taste
preferences may remain minor. For local image revision name affected card keys.
Do not submit the same rejected pixels with changed PNG metadata. Stop at limits;
do not spend calls repeatedly increasing detail/steps when the subject is wrong.

These rules support editorial review. They do not prove objective interest or
measure the false-acceptance rate of an independent judge.

## Bounded correction and independent identity

Current default: unrounded Q95 >=93.5 plus all card gates, initial image plus
at most three edits. A plateau (two gains below0.5 against the eligible best)
signals a strategy change, not early termination. Exhaust the remaining edits
unless accepted or a real budget/technical blocker intervenes. Discard a failed
card after the limit; a set missing one of ten accepted cards is rejected, not
quietly delivered as nine. No unbounded replan or counter reset.

Approve a recurring entity visually as a separate decision: readable full view,
identity, invariants, geometry, materials and exact source version. Food or
background faults outside the entity do not automatically reject its identity;
occlusion or faults within the entity do. Independently approved canon unblocks
dependent appearances even when its source card remains rejected. Never export
that card as accepted. Prefer a simple full-view neutral identity source over
a complex food interaction. Historical snapshots remain intact; explicit policy
amendments are versioned and preserve attempts.
